Transformers — Interactive Worksheet

A transformer is a device used to change the size of an alternating voltage or current.

Learning Objectives

By the end of this worksheet you will be able to:

  • Explain how a transformer works.
  • Describe how to increase or decrease output voltage.

Before You Start (Important!)

A transformer has:
  • Primary coil → connected to the power source
  • Secondary coil → where output voltage appears
  • No direct connection between the coils!

Part 1 — Getting Familiar with the Simulation

Open phet.colorado.edu, search for “Faraday’s Electromagnetic Lab”, open it, and select the Transformer tab.

  1. Turn ON “Show magnetic field”.
  2. Turn ON “Show electrons”.
  3. Click “AC power supply”.

Use the simulation below as a backup if PhET is unavailable.

Vp = 5.0 V Vs = 5.0 V Bulb: on
Hint

Think about the invisible thing that grows and shrinks around the coils.

Part 2 — What is happening in the first coil?

  1. Focus only on the primary coil (left side).
  2. Watch the electrons and magnetic field.

Part 3 — What happens in the second coil?

  1. Now look at the secondary coil (right side).
  2. Watch carefully when the magnetic field changes.

Part 4 — Test DC vs AC (Very Important!)

  1. Switch from AC → DC (use the “Source” button on the simulation above).
  2. Observe what happens.

Now switch back to AC.

Part 5 — Changing Number of Turns

  1. Keep the primary coil turns the same.
  2. Change secondary coil turns: try 5 → 10 → 20 loops.
More loops in secondary → voltage
Fewer loops in secondary → voltage

Part 6 — Changing Input Voltage

  1. Increase the voltage of the AC source.
  2. Observe the output.

Part 7 — Build Full Explanation

AC current in the primary coil creates a magnetic field.
This changing field induces in the secondary coil.
Show the formula (when you’re ready)

Vs / Vp = Ns / Np   — output voltage depends on the ratio of turns.
